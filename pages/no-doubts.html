No Doubts

80 × 100 cm
Oil on canvas
December, 2025

ABOUT THE WORK

How many times have you asked for advice, and followed your own path?

This work is about believing in yourself, trusting your gut feelings, your inner sense, and following your own “star.” Bold red strokes completely cover the linear drawing made just a few days earlier. The composition that once seemed correct and beautiful moves into the background, giving way to the artist’s passionate and fearless nature.

Here reside genuine emotions and the true energy of the moment.
