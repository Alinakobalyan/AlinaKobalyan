The Mirror

80 × 100 cm
Oil on canvas
September, 2025

ABOUT THE WORK

You look at a person as if in a mirror: first rejoicing in the reflection, then shattering it.

The artist invites you to pause and look within yourself, in silence and without witnesses. Dark blue tones with green transitions reflect calm and depth, drawing the viewer into this fragile space.

Bright neon bursts in the upper right corner hint at an exit from inner transformation — the light is already breaking through, brilliant and blinding.
