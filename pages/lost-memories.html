Lost Memories

80 × 100 cm
Oil on canvas
January, 2026

ABOUT THE WORK

A work about memories that shape us and make us unique. About lived experience that has solidified into a certain form, defining the final structure of one's personality. Like the rings of a tree trunk, where each year adds a new layer, a person accumulates memories over time.

Some memories are clear and consciously remembered; others remain deep within, not directly visible, yet continuing to influence the present.

Deep, multilayered dark tones, born from the blending of several colors, stand alongside pure, almost untouched pigments. It is a dialogue between the visible and the hidden, between what we remember and what has been lost or repressed.
