No Doubts

80 × 100 cm
Oil on canvas
September, 2025

ABOUT THE WORK

The work is an internal dialogue of the artist with circumstances. It reflects the observation of vivid expressions of beauty and greatness in people around, and the feeling of being detached from it.

Such brilliance can sting and cause pain, but only until it becomes part of you — just as alive, dangerous, full of movement and color. Each line of color is intensified by diffusion through broad, swift strokes. Like a peacock spreading its magnificent tail, we have the right to reveal every facet of ourselves, not hiding it, but enjoying and celebrating the divine beauty contained within.
