Yunus

80 × 100 cm
Oil on canvas
August, 2025

ABOUT THE WORK

A work inspired by the atmosphere of a love story in which events intertwine in a remarkable way, continually bringing us back to one another in new incarnations and revealing unexpected facets each time.

This piece is about encounters that were inevitably meant to happen, about a connection where freedom of movement and trust in God open new possibilities and breathe new life into what once seemed to have faded.

It speaks of trust and of savoring the process of life, embracing all its intricate storylines. Of a dance that turns out to be endless, where only the music changes, becoming louder or softer, faster or slower.
