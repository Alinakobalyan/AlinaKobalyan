About

Alina Kobalyan is an artist and architect based in Dubai. Originally trained in architecture at the Moscow Architectural Institute, she brings together visual art, spatial thinking, and an ongoing interest in human perception.

Working primarily with oil on canvas, Alina explores the emotional and intuitive dimensions of human experience. Her paintings investigate the relationship between colour, form, memory, and inner states, creating abstract compositions that invite personal interpretation.

Alongside her artistic practice, she founded Art Meditation, an independent creative project exploring the relationship between art, attention, and self-discovery through participatory drawing and immersive experiences.

Her practice is rooted in a curiosity about how we perceive and experience the world around us. Alina approaches art as a way of directing attention, opening new perspectives, and creating a dialogue between inner experience and external reality.

Whether through painting or shared creative experiences, her work invites viewers to slow down, look inward, and engage with the world in a more intuitive and attentive way.
