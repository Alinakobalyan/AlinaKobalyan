Save this fire for someone else

PAINTING · PERSONAL NARRATIVES

The paintings in this collection are energy, emotions, and steps toward healing, captured in color on canvas.

Each piece holds its own story and strength, inviting the viewer to look within and feel the living, fragile energy of the moment.

In this series, I combine personal experience, emotions, and the deep meanings of transformative journeys, turning them into a visual language accessible to everyone.

It is an honest dialogue with myself and the world, recorded in color, texture, and form.
